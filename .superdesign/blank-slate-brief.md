# Blank Slate Brief: AI Makeup Independent Site

## Do Not Reference Existing UI
Ignore the current implementation's layout, colors, cards, hero, spacing, and component styling. The existing UI is rejected. Keep only product requirements, SEO route coverage, bilingual support, and feature set.

## Product
- International AI makeup generator and virtual makeup try-on site.
- English first, Simplified Chinese supported.
- Main intent: users upload a selfie, choose makeup style, adjust intensity, generate before/after result, receive personalized face-fit notes.
- Commercial architecture: credits, subscriptions, saved projects, downloads, dashboard.

## Competitor Observations
- Lanta: direct upload tool and broad long-tail keyword coverage.
- Fotor: complete feature page and SEO explanation.
- GoStudio: online try-on categories for lips, eyes, full-face makeup.
- TheMakeupAI: close product positioning and tool matrix.

## Required Redesign
- Start from a blank canvas.
- Make it feel like a premium beauty brand and editorial makeup workstation.
- First viewport must communicate the actual product and include an immediately usable upload/try-on tool.
- Avoid all common AI visual tropes: purple/blue gradients, glowing orbs, glassy SaaS cards, generic dashboards.
- Use physical beauty cues: mirror, makeup tray, lipstick swatches, shade strips, face chart, editorial proof panels.
- Use high contrast black/ivory/cherry/chrome/taupe.
- Make the design memorable and conversion-focused.

## Pages To Support
- Home
- AI Makeup landing page
- AI makeup generator
- AI makeup filter
- Virtual makeup try-on
- What makeup suits my face AI
- Pricing
- Login
- Dashboard

## Recommended Direction
Beauty Counter OS: a full-screen online makeup counter with editorial face proof, upload drawer, cosmetic swatches, before/after verdict, and commerce-ready credit plan.
